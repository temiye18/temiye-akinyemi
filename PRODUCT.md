# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers evaluating Temiye for a full-time role, prospective clients weighing him for contract work, and peers in the developer community. They usually arrive skeptical and skim fast, on desktop or phone, deciding within seconds whether this person has real depth and taste. The site has to earn a second look, then reward it.

## Product Purpose

The personal portfolio of Temiye Akinyemi, a software engineer (3+ years, fullstack web and AI). It exists to establish credibility and craft at a glance and to open a conversation (a hire, a contract, a follow). Success is a visitor who leaves convinced he is a strong, considered engineer, remembers the site as distinctly his, and reaches out or keeps him in mind. Because he builds interfaces, the site itself is the primary evidence: every interaction is a live sample of the work.

## Brand Personality

Enigmatic, editorial, and restrained, with quiet confidence. The voice is plain and specific, first person, real work described in real words. No buzzwords, no hype, no em dashes. Emotionally it should intrigue first and reward on engagement: a visitor senses taste and depth before they have read a single case study.

## Anti-references

Never the generic Awwwards "creative developer" starter-pack: off-white plus giant grotesk, magnetic buttons, RGB-shift hover, kinetic marquees, rainbow tech logos, the same template motion everyone ships. The mechanics are fine; the templated, instantly-recognizable surface is the thing to avoid. Also avoid corporate SaaS flatness (stock blue, card grids, hero-metric tiles, buzzword copy) and gimmick-for-gimmick's-sake effects that tax the reader. If a stranger could say "AI or a template made that," it has failed.

## Design Principles

- **The medium is the proof.** Demonstrate the engineering through the site's own behavior; do not just claim it. If a screenshot could convey it, it is not pulling its weight.
- **Withhold, then reward.** Intrigue first, clarity and payoff on engagement. The enigma must always resolve into something legible and useful.
- **Spend spectacle once.** One signature moment per surface, restraint everywhere else. Loudness comes from motion, type, and contrast, not from adding color or effects.
- **Plain, specific voice.** Real projects in real words. No marketing language, no aphorisms, no em dashes.
- **Fast and accessible are non-negotiable.** Hold 60fps, honor reduced-motion, keep semantics and keyboard paths intact, and provide touch and mobile fallbacks. Craft must never tax the user.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast in both light and dark themes. `prefers-reduced-motion` is honored across every animation (smooth-scroll, shader, 3D orbit, reveals all degrade to static or instant). Pointer-only effects (custom cursor, magnetic, lens, tilt) are gated behind `(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)` with full functionality preserved for touch and keyboard. Visible focus states everywhere; a skip link to content; images carry meaningful alt text.
